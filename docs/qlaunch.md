# Qlaunch Takeover (replaced)

The qlaunch takeover described here in earlier versions (`qlaunch=0/1`, `HomeRestart Off`, `Home2Profile`) was replaced in beta119.00 by the **HOME daemon** and the **Home toggle** setting, and in beta121.00 by **Overlay mode**.

See **[HOME Toggle and Overlay Mode](home%20toggle%20and%20overlay.md)**.
